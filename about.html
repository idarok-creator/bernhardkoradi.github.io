ABOUT

BERNHARD KORADI

Artist · Photographer · Musician

I work across painting, photography and electronic music, moving between observation and construction, landscape and sound.

Each practice begins differently, but all are concerned with atmosphere, place and the experience of seeing and listening.

The paintings are made from direct observation — landscape, light and the slow registering of weather and time. The photographs continue that interest in place, often at the edge of clarity. The music, released as iDarok, is built in the studio: electronic, structured, and concerned with atmosphere in a different register.

One person. Three practices.
